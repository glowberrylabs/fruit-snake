# Plan: Fruit Snake

## Intent

- A small offline Android snake game for kids.
- Pick up and play. No reading required to play. No ads, accounts, or network.
- Every asset is bundled in the APK. The app requests no permissions at all,
  including no INTERNET permission.
- Short, friendly rounds with immediate replay.
- High score and mute preference persist across restarts.

## Stack and conventions

- Package and application id: `com.glowberrylabs.fruitsnake`.
- Root project name: `fruitsnake`.
- Kotlin 2.3.21, AGP 8.13.2, Compose BOM 2026.05.00, Gradle 8.14.5, JDK 21.
- minSdk 26, compileSdk 36, targetSdk 35.
- Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Compose Canvas for board rendering.
- CLI workflow through Gradle and `just`, following the conventions used by
  the existing Android repos.
- ktlint and detekt for formatting and static checks.
- Dual MIT/Apache-2.0 licensing.
- No game engine, Room, dependency injection framework, or navigation library.

## Game rules

- Board is 16 columns by 16 rows.
- Snake starts at length 3 in the center, facing right.
- One fruit is on the board at a time: apple, banana, or cherry.
- Every fruit is worth one point and grows the snake by one segment.
- Fruit spawns uniformly at a random empty cell and never on the snake.
- Initial tick is 320ms. Every 5 fruits reduces the tick by 15ms, with a
  minimum of 200ms.
- Up to two direction changes queue. A 180-degree turn relative to the last
  queued direction is rejected.
- Hitting a wall ends the round. Hitting the body ends the round.
- Moving into the current tail cell is safe when the snake is not eating,
  because the tail vacates it in the same tick.
- Filling the board wins. The maximum score is 253 with a starting length of 3.
- The high score is saved only when a round ends.

## Controls

- Swipe anywhere on the board. Direction is detected as soon as the drag
  crosses a threshold, not on release.
- Four large on-screen arrow buttons below or beside the board.
- Pause button in the HUD.
- Auto-pause when the app lifecycle reaches `ON_PAUSE`.
- Back pauses the game while a round is running. Another back press exits.
- Start, resume, and replay are large icon buttons.
- Keep the screen awake while a round is running.

## UI

- Light, playful theme with fixed brand colors, not dynamic color.
- Sky-to-cream vertical gradient background.
- Board framed by a rounded surface with a white border and soft shadow.
- Board cells alternate between two grass greens.
- Snake is drawn as overlapping circles with a head-to-tail green gradient,
  eyes oriented by the current direction, and a small pink forked tongue.
- Fruit uses bundled vector drawables with a soft shadow and a pop-in
  animation when it spawns.
- HUD shows a white pill with a fruit icon and the current count, a sound
  toggle, and a pause button. High score appears only on the start and
  round-end cards.
- Overlays for start, paused, game over, and win. Each uses a dimming scrim
  and a rounded card with a fade and scale animation.
- Friendly copy: "Nice try!", "New best!", and "You filled the board!".
- Score badge bounces when a fruit is eaten.
- Haptics on eat and on game over.
- Responsive layout: a column in portrait, and a row with the board on the
  left and HUD plus D-pad on the right in landscape.
- Edge-to-edge layout with safe drawing insets applied.

## Audio

- SoundPool with `USAGE_GAME` attributes.
- Two short WAV files in `res/raw`: one eat sound and one gentle game-over
  sound.
- Sounds are generated by `scripts/make_sounds.py` using the Python standard
  library, so there are no third-party audio assets.
- A mute toggle is persisted in SharedPreferences.
- Haptics use `View.performHapticFeedback`: `CLOCK_TICK` when eating and
  `LONG_PRESS` on game over.

## Assets

- Original vector XML for apple, banana, cherry, launcher foreground, and
  launcher monochrome.
- Adaptive launcher icon combining a snake head and an apple.
- No third-party art or fonts.
- No runtime network fetches.

## Architecture

- `game` package: pure Kotlin engine built around immutable `Game`,
  `GameConfig`, `Cell`, `Direction`, `Fruit`, `GameStatus`, and `SnakeEngine`.
- `data` package: `ScoreStore` wraps SharedPreferences for the high score and
  sound preference.
- `audio` package: `GameSounds` wraps SoundPool loading and playback.
- `ui` package: `FruitSnakeTheme`, `GameScreen`, `GameBoard`, `GameHud`,
  `Dpad`, `GameOverlays`, and fruit painter helpers.
- `GameViewModel`: owns the tick loop in `viewModelScope`, exposes UI state,
  accepts direction input, handles pause/resume/restart, and persists high
  score and sound settings.
- `MainActivity`: edge-to-edge setup, ViewModel wiring, lifecycle auto-pause,
  and back handling.
- `SnakeEngine` accepts an injectable `Random` so tests are deterministic.
- The UI depends only on immutable state and callbacks.

## Testing

- JUnit 4 unit tests for the engine:
  - initial snake and fruit placement
  - movement
  - growth and score when eating
  - new fruit never spawns on the snake
  - direction queue applies in order
  - opposite turns are rejected
  - wall death
  - self-collision death
  - tail cell is safe when not eating
  - speed ramp clamps at the minimum
  - win when the board fills
- `just check` runs ktlint and detekt.
- `just test` runs unit tests.
- `just build` produces the debug APK.
- `just run` installs and launches on the `pixel9a` emulator.
- Manual verification: all three fruits, pause, background and foreground,
  mute, high score persistence, and rotation.
- Capture `docs/screenshot.png` from the emulator and link it in the README.

## Non-features

- No internet permission, ads, analytics, accounts, or crash reporting.
- No levels, obstacles, bosses, power-ups, or multiplayer.
- No leaderboard or cloud sync.
- No custom skins or unlockables.
- No localization beyond English.
- No release signing configuration.
- No in-app purchases.
